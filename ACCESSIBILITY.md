# Accessibility

The Mosman Dry Eye site serves an optometry practice — many of our visitors
will have vision-related needs, so readability is a first-class requirement,
not an afterthought.

## Commitments
- Improve text visibility across all pages: higher contrast ratios and
  larger, clearer type (target: WCAG 2.1 AA — contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text)
- Verify every section at increased browser zoom and on small screens
- Keep decorative imagery from competing with informational text
